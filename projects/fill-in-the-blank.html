Fill in the Blank Generator

in use

A Chrome extension for English teachers. Highlight a text on any page and get a gap-fill worksheet with an answer key.

A photo of a textbook page. Snap it with a phone, click the words to hide, get the sheet and the key.

A text written for the lesson. Pick the level, the age and what to practise. The text is written in about five seconds.

No text at hand. Type a topic, and the extension finds an article on sites with free licences.
