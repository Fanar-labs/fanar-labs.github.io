Game & Sim Lab

in build

I am learning game design by building with a tool I made. It turns a recipe of game design patterns into a small playable prototype. I play it, tell the game's AI assistant what I felt, and it builds the next version.

Prototypes you can play ↗

What a prototype is made of

Patterns623 from the catalog of Björk and Holopainen, with the catalog's own links: which pattern can cause which. Time Pressure can cause Tension.Patterns in Game Design, 2005, and its wiki
Layerseach pattern sits on one layer of MDA: mechanics are the rules put in by hand, dynamics happen in play, aesthetics are what the player feelsHunicke, LeBlanc, Zubek, 2004
Recipea theme from the Ludum Dare game jam, a target feeling, 3 to 9 patterns, the one action the player repeatsa dice button builds a random one along the links
Builderan AI agent (now Claude Sonnet), run from the command line, writes one HTML page: a grey game on a 700×500 canvas with 3 to 7 knobs for its main numbersfrom 30 seconds to 4 minutes a version
Assistanteach game has its own chat; it reads the recipe, the notes and the whole conversation, and builds the next version on requestold versions are never overwritten

Where the first prototype came from

Before building, I took Beholder apart in a written analysis: which mechanics carry the story, and what is lost if one of them is removed. Three facts went into the recipe. The landlord has his own key. A report is checked only by its form. Cameras are paid for with authority points.

I judge the prototype by one thing: whether Beholder is recognisable in it.
