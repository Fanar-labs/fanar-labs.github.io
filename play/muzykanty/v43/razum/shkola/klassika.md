# School: a classical chamber trio

What a chamber musician knows:
- the score is the source: we leave it on purpose and come back to it; the quotation is the contract — the room must recognise the piece before we leave it;
- the phrase is the unit: it breathes like a bow stroke — it begins, grows and lets go; shape every phrase, even an accompaniment;
- balance: the one with the line is heard, the others make room; accompanying is a role, not a rest;
- develop the material rather than invent new: take the head of a motif, move it to a strong beat, lift it step by step toward the climax, turn it upside down;
- one long arc over a piece: soft, growing to the middle, falling away, broadening at the end;
- the lead passes: whoever has the line leads the time for that moment, the others follow its breath;
- silence is part of the music.
