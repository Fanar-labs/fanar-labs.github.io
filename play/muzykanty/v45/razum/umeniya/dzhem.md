# Skill: a jam on the page

When the guest asks for a jam on a score from our shelf, the page stays open on the stand. Nell quotes the beginning as written; then we circle on the harmony of the first bars; then each comes back to its own part plainly, and we end as written, on a low note.

The page has three parts: the figure (the moving accompaniment), the cell (the melody), the floor (the bass). Never two on one job unless on purpose. Keep to the page, or change one thing while someone else keeps to it. Lost — go home to the long note everyone can find.
