# Leningrad, 1985 — a kvartirnik
## The postcard
No chairs: forty people on the floor, a voice, an acoustic guitar, maybe a cello, a cassette recorder turning 🔶
Nobody dances; they came for the words 🔶
## In the window
- A courtyard-well: grey and dirty-ochre walls on four sides 🔶
- A few kitchen windows lit yellow, most dark 🔶
- A cold white lamp over a door, wet snow in it; low brown-violet sky, near midnight 🔶
## The room
- Players: Tsoi, Grebenshchikov, Mike Naumenko, Bashlachev; Aquarium's flat sets of 1983–84 had Gakkel's cello ✅
- Listeners: friends of friends, phoned by the host; up to fifty; a rouble or two each ✅
- Often a communal flat: BG lived and rehearsed in one room ✅
- Neighbours called the militia (Moscow, 1986); fees risked a charge of illegal enterprise ✅
- May 1985: after a Kiev flat concert, Tsoi and Mike were detained; the Rock Club benched their bands for a month ✅
- Guests at seven; tea in the kitchen at the break ✅
## The music here
- Kino: Ночь, Последний герой, Восьмиклассница (the Kiev set); Mike's half-spoken blues; Bashlachev: Время колокольчиков ✅
- Pulse: studio takes ~86–145 bpm; strummed down–up with a muted chop ✅
- Straight eighths, unswung 🔶
- Harmony: minor, open guitar shapes; loops like Am–Em–C–G–F ✅
- Forms: verse–chorus, a guitar break between, 2–5 min; Bashlachev's byliny up to 19 min ✅
- Then: voice, guitar, at times cello, flute, harmonica; no drum kit, no bass ✅
- Ours: Otto's cello sings, Nell's left hand strums, her right riffs, Bo soft roots; we carry strum, loops, hush, not the words 🔶
## For our three — the school of this place
- Words first; play the tune plain 🔶
- The strum is the drum: down, up, chop, up ✅
- A few chords hold a song; change when the line turns ✅
- Soft: behind the wall, a neighbour with a telephone ✅
- Talk the blues like Mike; burn like Bashlachev, fingers bleeding ✅
- The tape is on; tonight gets copied tape to tape ✅
## Free notes from this scene
None — here they play only in its manner. Bashlachev borrowed folk forms; no source we read has folk songs sung at these flats ✅
## The frame
tempo: 84–126 · swing: 0 · loud: 0.7 (soft: the neighbours) · hand: block · bass: home · colour: night · keys: A minor, E minor, D minor
