# Vienna, 1828 — a Schubertiade
## The postcard
Winter, a crowded flat: Schubert at the fortepiano, Vogl singing beside him; after supper, dancing past midnight ✅
## In the window
- Blue-black winter night (Dec 1826, Jan 1828) ✅
- Dim yellow oil street lamps ✅
- Narrow lane, grey snow, pale houses, yellow-lit windows, St Stephen's spire black 🔶
## The room
- 1825–27 in general: hosts Spaun, Schober, Witteczek; officials, poets, painters, wives; readings ✅
- 15 Dec 1826, Spaun's: Vogl sang ~30 songs, Schubert and Gahy four hands; supper, dancing; 12:30, merry, to a tavern ✅
- 1828 itself: Jan, Spaun's engagement party: Bocklet, Schuppanzigh, Linke play a new Schubert trio ✅
- Context only: 26 Mar 1828, his one public concert of his own works ✅
- Songs soft, dances louder 🔶
## The music here
- Played: songs; Winterreise (1827; sung by Schubert to friends; printed from Jan 1828); four-hand marches; Écossaises, Valses nobles for dancing ✅
- Pulse: songs slow–moderate, dances 3/4 (Ländler, waltz) or 2/4 (Écossaise); ~56–120 🔶
- Harmony: flat keys; sudden turns a third away ✅; major/minor shading 🔶
- Forms: Lied, cycle, dance chains, march with trio, piano trio ✅
- Instruments: Viennese fortepiano (leather hammers, moderator), voice; strings if players came ✅. Ours: Otto sings, Nell is Schubert; four hands: Nell primo, Otto+Bo secondo; no bass here: Bo takes the left hand's bass or rests; we lose poems and dancing 🔶
## For our three — the school of this place
- The singer leads; Schubert felt he and Vogl were one ✅
- Ornament lightly; Vogl's seemed mannered ✅
- After supper, dances for whole nights; improvise more ✅
- A march trio moved a guest almost to tears ✅
- Let the harmony turn a third away, then come home ✅
