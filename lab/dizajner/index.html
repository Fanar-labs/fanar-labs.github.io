How to use this

  
Two screens: the Constructor, where you assemble a game's recipe, and the Stand, where you play the prototype built from it.

  
    
    
  1. Recipe — in the center. A theme, the goal (what the player should feel) and cards — patterns from the catalog on three shelves: feelings, dynamics, mechanics. Arrows show what can cause what.
    2. 
    
  2. 🎲 Reroll changes every unlocked card; the new ones lead to the goal. 🔒 keeps a card, ↶ undo brings the recipe back.
    3. 
    
  3. Left — where cards come from: a tree from the goal down — «what can cause it». Click a card in the recipe and the tree shows what can cause that card. «+» puts a card on its layer.
    4. 
    
  4. Right — the output: the recipe exactly as the worker gets it. ⚡ Generate builds the prototype and opens the Stand.
    5. 
    
  5. Stand — play, ● Start test, and talk to the game's design assistant.
    6. 
  

  
Built on: the catalog Patterns in Game Design
    (Björk & Holopainen, book 2005, wiki) · MDA
    (Hunicke, LeBlanc, Zubek, 2004) · jam themes of Ludum Dare · nine steps from Fullerton's
    Game Design Workshop.
    The catalog marks a layer for only 224 patterns; the layer of every pattern here, and which patterns express each goal, are our reading of the pages.
